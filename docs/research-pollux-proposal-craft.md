## 1. Written proposal (official topics)

English PDF or Word. Max 10MB. Cover these eight topics in the body (UNESCO required headings, any order they name; we use §9 order):

1. **Team.** Up to 6 people, ages 18-30. Affiliation and country. Equal billing: Pollux (product) and Seekers Guild (community). Copy names from the coordinator roster when it exists.
2. **Problem statement.** Saturday outreach with no agenda, no handouts, no URL students keep. UNESCO MIL facts as context only.
3. **Objectives.** RBM-lite. SMART and modest. See §6.
4. **Target audience.** Primary: SK / LGU youth officers (Mia). Secondary: students who keep the URL; Seekers Guild partners outside Manila.
5. **Prototype or concept.** Open kit: six modules, 3Ds run-of-show, print packet, student reader URL. Demo: SK can run Saturday from the bag.
6. **Sustainability.** Materials stay free. OSS stewardship. SK can rerun without a Guild workshop. Hosting path for the student site.
7. **Creativity.** Officer-run session plus keepable URL; partner community that does not speak for the Page.
8. **Feasibility.** Kit already in `docs/outreach-kit/`. One Saturday. Phone-first. No paid vendor.

## 6. RBM-lite (objectives)

Keep activities in Prototype. Keep outcomes in Objectives.

An **activity** is a thing Mia does with the kit (print the packet, teach a module, send the URL).

An **outcome** is a change after she does it, stated so a Saturday could show it.

Write objectives that are SMART and modest:

- Specific: SK officer runs the session from the kit.
- Measurable: session completed from the packet; students received a URL they can reopen.
- Achievable: one barangay Saturday, existing six modules.
- Relevant: MIL practice in the session (pause, source, ethical share).
- Time-bound: this Saturday plus the week students still have the URL.
