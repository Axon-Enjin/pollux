## 1. Roles (do not collapse)

Mia runs the session. She is SK. Saturday outreach is hers.

Pollux packs the bag. The bag is the open kit: modules, a run-of-show, a logistics list, a site students can open after.

Seekers Guild partners with SK and LGU on the same materials, including outside Manila. Guild never speaks for her Page.

---

## 2. Tone

Serious. Matter-of-fact. Short sentences.

Competence plus sincerity. Name the scene: Saturday outreach, no agenda, modules, run-of-show, student URL.

Write like a colleague packing the bag, not like an explainer video. No FAQ headings. No definition by saying what Pollux is not. Periods and commas only for pauses. No U+2014. No spaced en-dash used as a pause.

---

## 3. We say

Use these words in public copy:

- Mia
- SK
- Saturday outreach
- modules
- run-of-show
- logistics
- student URL
- Seekers Guild partner
- outside Manila
- they run the session / we pack the bag

Prefer her action: she runs the session, she teaches the modules, she tailors the run-of-show, she sends students the URL.

---

## 4. We do not say

Do not write "only her tap makes it SK-official" as the product. Official-pack tap is a later side tool.

Do not write the fake map as the product. The scene is Saturday outreach with no agenda.

Do not paywall materials. Nothing valuable is paywalled.

Do not put Guild on the SK Page. Guild partners. Guild does not speak for her Page.

Do not use explainer headings: What-is-[product], How it works, a Bottom-Line closer, FAQ headings.

Do not cold-open with an Imagine-someone scene. Start with Mia, Saturday, or the blank outreach.

Do not write purpose copy in the "designed to" plus help pattern.

Do not brand Pollux as a free, catch-all digital utility.

Do not use U+2014 or a spaced en-dash as punctuation.
