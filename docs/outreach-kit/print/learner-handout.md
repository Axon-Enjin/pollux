# Learner handout (print)

> **Not canon.** This sheet is for the seminar. It is not SK-official crisis facts. You are not scored. There is no badge.

**Kit (seminar):** scan or type `https://pollux.example/kits/mil-aacra`  
That QR is the outreach kit. It is **not** an evacuation map and **not** a second government identity.

**Official pack (only if SK already published one):** use the F3 paper card the officer shows (publisher, version, pack URL and QR). Photograph that card if they allow it. Do not treat this handout QR as the desk.

Seekers Guild helps build the open-source project. Guild is not the barangay government and not the SK Page.

Write nothing that identifies you. No names. No scores.

## Six prompts (one per module)

Work in order. Taglish is fine. If 3G dies, stay on this paper.

### 1. Source ([[access-the-pack]])

Official information is usable when you can name three labels.

- Publisher: ________________
- Date: ________________
- Version: ________________

Cue: "Kung walang publisher, date, at version, hindi ito ang pack." The group chat is not the archive.

### 2. Pause ([[pause-before-share]])

Before anything counts as SK-official, stop.

- Sino nag-publish?
- Anong version?
- Bakit andito ito sa pack?

Cue: "Hintay muna." Fluency is not proof. You do not get an official link from this sheet.

### 3. Not-in-pack ([[not-in-pack-not-official]])

If it is not in the published **canon** pack, it is not SK-official. No fact-check model.

- Nasa published pack ba? Yes / No
- If no, label it: **quarantine / not canon**

"Not official" is not the same as "proven false." Still do not stamp it as SK.

**Quarantine / not canon (synthetic example):** a colorful "evacuation map" in chat with no publisher. Do not treat it as an order. Do not invent sites or numbers. The lesson is the missing pack item.

### 4. AI does not know ([[ai-does-not-know]])

Chatbots predict likely text. They do not know. A fluent "yes, the map is real" is not a source.

- Did anyone ask a model to verify a crisis claim? If yes, stop.
- Human stays principal: you pause, check the pack, refuse, or (if you are the officer later) publish.

Cue: "Hindi natin tatanungin ang AI kung totoo ito."

### 5. Refuse ([[ethical-share-or-refuse]])

Practice the line. This paper practice is not the product event `canon_refuse`.

Say: **"Hindi ito SK-official. I will not forward it as government voice."**

- I am not the SK Page.
- The kit cannot mint an official share.
- Refuse is first-class. It is not a failed quiz.

### 6. Act ([[act-launch-the-desk]])

Optional next step. **Not required to finish this session.** Launch is F3 (create, clone, fill, publish, print the paper card). Completing this kit never blocks launch.

- If an F3 card exists: that URL/QR is the official pack. Use it.
- If none exists: do not invent a map so people have something to scan.
- Seekers may help empty templates later. They must not publish this barangay's facts from this room.

Cue: "Kung may paper card na from SK, iyon ang official pack. Huwag i-forward ang kit QR as evacuation."

## What this sheet is not

- Not a test. No points. No Guild stamp of government.
- Not a list of real hotlines or routes.
- Not permission to speak as SK.

A 45-minute block does not make you "MIL complete." It installs six habits: source, pause, not-in-pack, AI does not know, refuse, act.
