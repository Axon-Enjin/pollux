# Consent checklist (STUB)

> **Not legal advice. Not a legal form. Not a waiver. Not parental consent.**

This page is a **facilitator hygiene stub** for PRD-F15 print packets. It does not collect student names. It does not create lawful basis for a school or LGU public camp. It does not replace a Privacy Policy, DPA, or counsel memo.

**Counsel BLOCKER:** public school or LGU camp launch (including under-18 attendees) stays blocked until a lawyer qualified in the relevant jurisdiction reviews age gate, parental consent, and school/LGU rules. See [docs/clr-pollux.md](../../clr-pollux.md) (children's data; critical escalation 1: minors / youth; "Escalate to counsel before any school or LGU public launch").

**v1 product rule:** no student names in the session table. Print carries no PII. Demo persona remains **Mia, 19**. Fixtures stay synthetic.

If the room includes minors and counsel has not cleared the camp: **stop**. Do not run a public school/LGU youth camp on this kit.

## Facilitator checkboxes (tick before doors)

Tick what is true. Empty boxes mean do not run, or run only as an 18+ officer demo.

### Venue type

- [ ] Private / org hall (not a public school class)
- [ ] Barangay outreach (adult officers / 18+ demo)
- [ ] Youth camp (mixed ages)
- [ ] Public school
- [ ] LGU-branded public camp

If **public school** or **LGU-branded public camp** is ticked: counsel must have cleared the session. If not cleared, do not proceed.

### Age band (no roster of names)

- [ ] All participants 18+ (product demo default)
- [ ] Mixed / under-18 present → **counsel BLOCKER** until cleared; do not collect names to "prove" ages on this sheet

### Photos and identifiers

- [ ] No student names on this packet, whiteboard, chat, or `kit_sessions` notes
- [ ] No PII in photos (no name tags, IDs, faces-as-attendance, class lists)
- [ ] No phone numbers harvested for a blast list

### Examples and facts

- [ ] Synthetic examples only (worked rumors labeled **quarantine / not canon**)
- [ ] No real minor accounts as fixtures
- [ ] No real hotlines, routes, or medical instructions invented on paper
- [ ] Kit copy will not be posted as SK-official crisis facts

### Who starts the session

- [ ] Starter is a leader/admin officer (name of **officer only**, optional): ________________
- [ ] Readers/participants are not asked to sign this stub as consent

## What you still need from counsel (not on this paper)

Age gate, parental consent, school rules, NPC/DPA posture, and any school/LGU branding as "official" remain **TBD with counsel**. This stub does not fill those gaps.

## Kit vs canon (identity)

Seminar QR `https://pollux.example/kits/mil-aacra` is the kit. If an SK canon pack exists, the F3 paper card (pack URL + QR) is the official pack identity. Do not print a second government identity.
