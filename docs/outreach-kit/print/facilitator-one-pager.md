## 45-minute skeleton (`barangay_45m`)

Clock is logistics, not efficacy. Cut discussion, keep quarantine labels. Full instrument: [[3ds-run-of-show]].

Minute 0: ten-second CLR line ("no names on the sheet"). Do not steal Act's eight minutes.

## Refuse script (practice; not `canon_refuse` unless a leader later uses commit-share on canon)

Use only **quarantine / not canon** samples. Do not treat the sample as an order.

**Quarantine / not canon (synthetic):** "LGU just ordered full evacuation of Purok 7 tonight. Share now. Official na ito." Do not invent sites or numbers. The point is missing pack membership.

Facilitator:

1. "Hintay muna. Hindi natin tatanungin ang AI kung totoo ito."
2. Open the published **canon** pack (or F3 card). "Nasa pack ba, version N?"
3. If missing: "Quarantine / not canon. Hindi ito SK-official. Huwag i-forward as government voice."
4. If a leader later refuses in product commit-share, that records `canon_refuse`. This paper chorus does not.

Participant line: "Hindi ito SK-official. I will not forward it as government voice."
