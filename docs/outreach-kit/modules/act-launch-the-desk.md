# Act, launch the desk

> Act here is an optional walk through the SK self-launch checklist: create org, clone a flood template, fill local fields, publish, print the paper card. Completing this module is never a launch gate.

## Definition

**Act, launch the desk** is the last outreach-kit module. It points at **PRD-F3**, the SK self-launch kit: a launch checklist (create, clone, fill, publish, post, print) and a one-page **paper card** with the canon pack URL and QR. The walk is optional. A deep-link into org create or the checklist is allowed when the product is live. The session must not require that walk.

This is AACRA **Act** as product behavior: informed, ethical media habits that close the loop in public, not a badge. UNESCO's "build a better society / fairer public talk" reading in Pollux is a local desk the officer can launch herself. It is not a Guild workshop requirement.

This module is **not** PRD-F3 itself. F3 is the product surface. This note is facilitator copy in `pack_kind = outreach_kit`. Kit text is not SK-official crisis canon. This module is **not** PRD-F12 (optional scored inoculation drill). Completing F12 or F15 must not block US-01 launch.

Seekers may help empty templates, issues, and code without publishing that barangay's facts. Guild is not the SK Page brand. Guild is not required to launch.

## Why It Works

Tips without a complete job stay theater. The smallest complete SK job Pollux owns is launch-without-us: create org, clone template, fill local fields, publish, copy link or print card, post from the Page. That is F3. A camp can *show* that job. It must not *require* it.

The optional walk works because:

1. **Skin is on the desk, not the kit.** Publishing a canon pack is a human principal action. A `kit_module_opened` event is not `canon_share`.
2. **Empty fields force locality.** Clone leaves routes and contacts empty. Fill before publish is the product gate. The seminar does not invent those fields.
3. **Paper survives 3G.** The F3 paper card is the canon identity for a published pack. The kit print packet is pedagogy. They must not become two government identities.
4. **Contribution is not speech.** Seekers can improve CC-BY template shells. They do not speak as that SK.

Do not claim the walk "creates initiative." Research claim **AE-3** (initiative requires practiced Act with skin in the game, not tips alone) is **Unverified** as a Pollux causal claim. Teach the checklist. Do not sell the drill as the cause of later posting.

## Worked Example (youth camp close)

**Setting:** Last beat of a youth camp or barangay outreach. Facilitator has a published `outreach_kit`. Two SK desks in the room may differ: one already published a **canon** flood pack; one has not. Kit and canon stay two allowlists.

**Activity (about 8 to 12 minutes, not scored, not required to leave):**

1. Recap the sequence: labeled pack ([[access-the-pack]]), pause, refuse if not in pack ([[not-in-pack-not-official]]), then ethical share or refuse ([[ethical-share-or-refuse]]). Act is "the desk exists because a leader published," not "we all post now."
2. **If that SK already has a published canon pack:** point at the **F3 paper card** (pack URL and QR). Participants may photograph the card. That card is the canon identity. Do not mint `canon_share` from the kit runner. Optional deep-link: open the live pack read. Do not force login.
3. **If that SK has no published canon pack:** print the **kit packet only**. Do not print a fake paper card. Do not invent routes, sites, or numbers so the camp "has something to scan."
4. Optional walk (leaders only, if they choose): org create → clone flood template → fill local fields → publish → paper card. Incomplete required fields: publish denied. Completing this beat is **not** required to launch later from a phone at home.
5. Seekers in the room: show CONTRIBUTING rungs. They may file a template gap. They must not type that barangay's hotline into a public PR.

**Quarantine / not canon.** A volunteer prints a "Camp Act certificate" with a QR to the kit URL and tells the hall it is the official evacuation map. Label that artifact **quarantine / not canon**. Kit QR is not a second government identity. A fluent camp handout is not a published flood pack.

No real hotlines. No LLM-filled local fields. A 45-minute close does not launch the municipality.

## When It Breaks

- **Launch gate.** Facilitator or product requires `kit_module_opened` or F12 score before publish. Violates US-09 and launch-without-us.
- **Fake paper card.** Kit packet QR presented as SK canon. Two identities. US-10 forbids this.
- **Filled demo as live facts.** Facilitator types invented routes into a clone "for the demo" and publishes. That is false canon, not teaching.
- **Seeker as publisher.** A non-leader publishes that barangay's facts from the camp laptop. Role check must 403; pedagogy must say no.
- **AE-3 overclaim.** "You did the Act drill, so you will post tonight." Unverified causal claim. Stop.
- **F12 mix-in.** Scoring, badges, or `attempts` on this path. This is not the inoculation game.
- **Commit from kit.** Kit session mints `canon_share`. RFC dual allowlist forbids it. Official share stays on published canon after human commit ([[ethical-share-or-refuse]]).
- **Guild chrome on the Page.** Launch walk brands the SK Page as Seekers Guild. Product failed the three-role cut line.
- **3G death without a rule.** If the deep-link fails, fall back to paper: existing F3 card or kit packet only. Do not improvise crisis lists.

## Hidden Assumptions

- F3 exists as a product path (checklist + paper card) even when `ENABLE_OUTREACH_KIT` is off. This module is Should-Have pedagogy. Launch does not wait for it.
- Optional deep-link targets org create / clone / checklist, not a crisis fact endpoint and not an LLM coach.
- "Fill local fields" means the SK officer's real routes and sources, later, on a device they control. Camp time may only *name* the steps.
- Someone with `leader` (or admin) may publish. Readers participate. Do not invent a fourth role.
- Empty CC-BY templates are not canon. Published SK facts are not copied into the public repo by default.
- AACRA Act includes advocacy and reporting abuse in Lizette's hero story. This module does not teach doxxing, pile-ons, or Page blasting. Act here is launch or refuse, with skin on a human publish.
- AE-3 remaining Unverified means we may still *offer* a complete job. We may not claim the offer causes initiative.

## Application

Facilitator checklist:

- Open only published `outreach_kit` for this module's notes.
- Say out loud: "Hindi required mag-launch para matapos ang session. Launch is F3. Hindi gate ang kit."
- If a published canon pack exists, point at the F3 paper card. If not, kit packet only.
- Optional deep-link for leaders who *ask*: create org → clone flood template → fill local fields → publish → paper card. Do not fill fields for them. Do not publish for them.
- Seekers: "Pwede kayong tumulong sa templates. Hindi kayo mag-publish ng facts ng barangay na 'to."
- Record `kit_module_opened` if a session is running. Do not mint `canon_share`. Do not write `attempts`.
- Close: "Act is the desk, kung pipiliin ninyo. Official share is still a human commit on canon ([[ethical-share-or-refuse]])."

Participant-safe cues:

- "Kung may paper card na from SK, iyon ang official pack URL. Kung wala, huwag mag-imbento ng mapa."
- "Huwag i-forward ang kit QR as evacuation."
- Readers do not get facilitator notes. Readers are not scored.

## Ethical Caution

Do not pressure youth to post from an SK Page. Do not pressure anyone to create an org in the hall. Do not collect extra youth PII for a "launch certificate." Consent and under-18 rules stay in CLR; this module is not a waiver.

Do not claim UNESCO certification, Guild authority, or that a short Act beat produces initiative (AE-3 Unverified). Do not recite crisis procedures from kit copy. If a participant is in distress, stop and follow the host organization's human process. No LLM. No open-web scrape.

Kit never blocks pack launch. Seekers helping templates must keep local fields empty. SK-owned facts stay off the public repo unless that SK contributes a sanitized shell.
