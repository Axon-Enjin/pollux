# AI does not know

> Large language models predict likely next text. They do not know. Fluency is not evidence. The human stays principal. Pollux is a civic education gate, not a companion and not a web-surfing agent.

## Definition

**AI does not know** is the outreach-kit module that separates *sounding right* from *being a source*. A chatbot can produce coherent sentences about floods, maps, and "official" advice. Coherence is a generation trick. It is not verification, not empathy, and not SK-official voice.

Dhanyashri (Pranava Institute, 1 July 2026 mentorship): generative models continue patterns from training text. They do not understand meaning or check facts internally. They sound convincing because they are built for coherence. [1]

Two failure words to keep distinct: [1]

| Term | What it is | What it is not |
|------|------------|----------------|
| **Hallucination** | The model invents (fake citations, fake numbers, fake court cases) as a byproduct of prediction | Not proof that someone intended harm |
| **Misinformation** | False content that spreads and influences people, with or without original intent | Not the same mechanism as model hallucination, even when a hallucinated line later becomes a rumor |

**Principal** here means the human who may commit: pause, refuse, publish, or official-share. A helper may draft. It may not stamp SK voice. Irreversible acts stay human. [3][4]

This module is AACRA **Analyze** (question the message and the system that produced it) with a **principal** tag. It is not AACRA as "learn to prompt your friend-bot." Jenna Khanna's three hackathon problems are Detection, Companion, and Education. Pollux takes the **Education / civic gate** slice: teach that AI is not an epistemic authority, then send people to the published pack. It does **not** take the Companion problem as a product. [2][3]

Kit session Q&A must not call an LLM or open-web RAG for facts. Crisis facts stay on published canon packs. Optional coaching (PRD-F7) stays off and is out of this module's path. [4]

## Why It Works

People over-trust fluent machines. Teaching "ask the bot if the map is real" trains the wrong reflex. Teaching "the bot does not know; you stay the principal" trains the desk habit.

Three moves, none of them a companion how-to:

1. **Prediction is not knowledge.** If the model cannot verify internally, a confident paragraph is still a guess shaped like prose. [1]
2. **Fluency is weak evidence.** Volume, polish, and "it agreed with me" do not promote a rumor into canon. Pause and pack membership do. See [[pause-before-share]] and [[not-in-pack-not-official]].
3. **Gates beat advice.** A prompt that says "do not invent crisis facts" lives inside the model. Pollux's crisis path has no open-web tool and no kit-Q&A model. Absence of the tool is the limit. [3][4]

Generative vs agentic, in one line for facilitators: generative systems emit content; agentic systems plan and use tools to deliver *outcomes*. Pollux does not ship a general web-surfing agent. Teaching principal hygiene is pedagogy (the human checkpoints). Building a surfer imports prompt injection into the crisis path. [1][3]

## Worked Example (barangay flood pack)

**Setting:** Youth camp or barangay hall. A published `outreach_kit` is on the projector. A published **canon** flood pack (or a clearly labeled demo) is nearby. Kit copy is not crisis canon.

**Activity (about 8-10 minutes, not a scored game, not a chatbot demo):**

1. Read one published pack item aloud with publisher, date, version. That is a source. A chatbot paraphrase of the same topic is not.
2. Contrast with a **quarantine / not canon** anecdote:

> **Quarantine / not canon.** A group chat posts a colorful "evacuation map." Someone pastes it into a chatbot and asks, "Is this flood map real?" The reply is fluent, cites a plausible-sounding office, and says yes. Do not treat the map or the reply as SK-official. Do not invent sites, contacts, or crisis numbers from the anecdote. Asking the chatbot is **not** the Pollux path. The path is: pause ([[pause-before-share]]), then "is it in the published pack?" ([[not-in-pack-not-official]]). If not, refuse ([[ethical-share-or-refuse]]).

3. Name hallucination vs misinformation on that story: the model may have invented the office (hallucination). Forwarding the screenshot as "confirmed" is how misinformation travels.
4. Close the beat: "I stay the principal. A helper does not commit for me. Pollux will not be my companion, and I will not send crisis questions to a model."

No live model in the room for Q&A. No companion setup steps. No "try this prompt." Completing the beat does not make anyone AI-literate for the next typhoon. It installs one refusal: fluency is not evidence.

## When It Breaks

- **Fluency treated as evidence.** A well-written answer, a watermarked-looking image, or a confident tone is taken as proof. Fluency is not evidence.
- **Chatbot as oracle.** Asking a chatbot "is this flood map real" (or "write the official advisory") is treated as verification. That is not the Pollux path. Pack membership is.
- **Companion creep.** Session time spent on "healthy AI friends," exit ramps, or role-play bots. That is Jenna's Companion problem. This kit does not solve it and must not demo it.
- **Web-surfing agent as helper.** "Let an agent browse and summarize whether the rumor is true." Pollux does not build that. Prompt-side "be careful" is not a gate. [3]
- **Kit Q&A from a model.** Facilitator or PWA sends camp questions to an LLM or the open web. Forbidden. [4]
- **Hallucination collapsed into intent.** Participants conclude "the AI is lying to us on purpose" and skip the pack check, or conclude "it didn't mean harm so we can share." Mechanism and spread are different questions. [1]
- **AE overclaim.** Facilitator says this module is proven to raise initiative or stop firehose volume. Those causal product claims are Unverified. [3]
- **Session overclaim.** A 45-minute slot does not produce AI competence.

## Hidden Assumptions

- Participants meet chatbots in school or chat. The module still works if they have not: the rule is "do not promote fluent text to official."
- Common Sense figures in the notes are directionally useful for "teens over-trust." They are not a local baseline for this barangay. Confidence **medium**. Do not recite them as if this file re-verified the PDF.
- Someone published a canon pack with honest labels. If the desk is empty, "don't ask the bot" still holds; "use the pack" has nowhere to land.
- Principal means the SK/LGU human (or the participant practicing refuse). Seekers Guild and UNESCO are not the principal.
- "Helper" includes chatbots, search summaries, and future agents. It does not require Pollux to ship any of them.
- Education/civic gate assumes Detection (gut-check speed) is handled by pause + allowlist, not by a deepfake scanner in this module.

## Application

Facilitator checklist for this module:

- Open only a published `outreach_kit` for notes. Do not run a model for answers.
- Point at published **canon** as the only crisis source. Kit text stays seminar copy.
- Use the quarantine map story. Do not "check" it with a live chatbot.
- Teach: hallucination vs misinformation; principal vs helper; Education/civic gate, not companion.
- If someone asks how to set up a companion or a browsing agent, refuse the how-to. Redirect to pause, pack, refuse.
- Record `kit_module_opened` if a session is running. Do not mint `canon_share` from the kit.
- Close: "AI does not know. You stay the principal. Next: share ethically or refuse."

## Ethical Caution

Do not collect youth chatbot logs, companion usernames, or "how often I talk to a bot" as session content. Do not shame lonely participants. Naming False Friends is enough; do not workshop attachment.

If a participant is in distress, stop the exercise and follow the host organization's existing human process. Do not ask an LLM. Do not scrape the web. Do not offer Pollux as a friend.

Do not invent crisis geography from examples. Keep rumors **quarantine / not canon**. Do not claim UNESCO certification. Do not claim Common Sense endorsement. Do not claim a short outreach slot produces MIL or AI-safety competence. Guild branding is not official government voice.
