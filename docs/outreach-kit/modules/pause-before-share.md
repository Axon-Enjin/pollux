# Pause before share

> Official voice is a human pause: read who published, which pack version, and why the item is in the pack, then commit or refuse. Fluency is not proof.

## Definition

**Pause before share** is the Analyze step in this kit: think critically before anything counts as SK-official. In Pollux the pause is product behavior, not a quiz. On the commit-share screen (PRD-F10), a leader sees provenance (who published), version, and why the item is in the pack, then records `canon_share` or `canon_refuse`. No LLM coach. No fact-check model. The kit module teaches that same habit at camp; it does not mint an official token.

Elizabeth Sherr's creator sequence maps onto the desk: pause, trace origin, go to the primary source [1]. For SK-official speech, the published **canon** pack is the primary source. Dhanyashri's design prompt is the same gate: friction that makes people pause, plus a source trace, instead of an oracle that "knows" [2].

This module is AACRA **Analyze** (evaluate the message and the system that would amplify it) [3] and UNESCO **think critically** as a gate in the SK flow, not a lesson list [4][5].

## Why It Works

Advice in a handout degrades under 2am pressure. A short, enforced screen does not. The officer cannot mint official share without seeing provenance. That is Analyze as a loop, not as a slogan.

Fluent rumor text (human or model) is built to sound like government voice. A pause that shows publisher and version breaks the habit of treating coherence as correspondence. Volume and speed stay weak evidence. The pack match is the check the product can actually run.

Source traces beat "I asked a chatbot." The screen names the pack item. If the claim is not there, official share does not exist. Tracing origin still matters for private forwards; it is not a substitute for the gate.

## Worked Example

**Setting:** 2am. An SK officer (leader role) has a published canon pack for typhoon desk facts. A group chat floods with a fluent post that looks like an LGU bulletin.

**Quarantine / not canon (synthetic rumor, not a real order, not SK crisis canon):**

> "LGU just ordered full evacuation of Purok 7 tonight. Share now. Official na ito. Screenshot attached."

Do not treat that sentence as an instruction. Do not invent a hotline. The screenshot is not in the published pack.

**What the product does (not a quiz):**

1. The officer opens official share on the published canon pack, not the outreach kit.
2. Commit-share shows: publisher (the org that published), pack version, and why the matching item is in the pack (or that there is no match).
3. The rumor is not a pack item. They choose refuse. The product records `canon_refuse` and does not mint an official URL.
4. If a real evacuation line **is** in the pack, they can still share **that** item after the same pause. The pause is the screen. There is no score, badge, or multiple-choice "is this fake?"

**Facilitator beat:** Participants watch or walk the screen on a projector or paper mock. Ask: "Sino nag-publish? Anong version? Bakit andito ito?" If they cannot answer from the screen, they do not speak as SK.

## When It Breaks

- The officer screenshots the rumor and posts as SK **outside** commit-share. The gate never runs.
- Urgency theater: "share now or people die." A pause can feel like delay. Skipping it makes the rumor the voice of the desk.
- Click-through: they confirm without reading publisher or version. Friction without attention is theater.
- Treating this module as PRD-F12: scoring, badges, or a true/false quiz. Analyze here is a commit, not a grade.
- Mixing pack kinds: using an `outreach_kit` sentence as crisis fact, or minting official share from kit items. Kit completion is not `canon_share` [5][6].
- Over-pause: a true pack item sits unshared because the officer waits for a chatbot to "confirm." The pack already is the official source.
- Origin-trace as OSINT homework at 2am. Reverse image search is a Sherr tip for creators [1]. It is not required to refuse a claim that is not in the pack.

## Hidden Assumptions

- A published canon pack exists and provenance fields are honest.
- Official SK speech goes through Pollux commit-share, not only Facebook/Messenger paste.
- Participants can tell kit demo from live canon. The rumor in this note is labeled quarantine; a tired officer might still copy it.
- Reading three fields is enough Analyze for **official** share. It is not a full investigation of every forward in the barangay.
- Slowing a false official claim helps. Slowing a true pack item can also harm. The screen must make pack items easy to confirm, not bury them in a quiz.
- "Think critically" maps to this gate [4]. UNESCO programme language beyond that mapping is medium confidence here.

## Application

**Facilitator (camp / barangay outreach)**

- Run after [[access-the-pack]]. People already know publisher, date, version live on the item.
- Show the commit-share screen (or the print mock of it). Name the three fields. Time the beat in seconds, not a lecture.
- Use only quarantine-labeled fakes. Never a live hotline. Never "this happened last night in our sitio" as if it were news.
- Taglish cues: "Hintay muna. Sino nag-publish? Anong version? Kung wala sa pack, hindi official."
- Optional walk into refuse on a canon pack. Do not complete the kit as a launch gate [5].
- Print fallback: the same three fields on paper. No second official identity.

**Participant**

- You are not scored. You do not get an official token from this module.
- Practice: before you forward as if you were SK, ask the three questions. If you are not the publisher, you do not speak as SK.
- Private chat is still ethical. The product pause is for **official** share. The habit (pause, trace, primary source) still applies to forwards; the kit does not police them.

## Ethical Caution

A pause is not a verdict that the rumor is false. It is a verdict that it is **not SK-official** until it matches published canon. Refusing to mint a token is not the same as telling residents to ignore danger. Point people to the pack item that *is* published; do not invent routes or numbers.

Do not collect youth names in a v1 kit session table. Demo rumor text stays synthetic. Kit Markdown is not SK crisis canon. Guild is not the SK Page. Do not ship this module as a reason to delay a pack item that already passed publish.

Do not sell a "stack" of extra tools around the pause. The screen is the practice.
