## Problem (MIL)

UNESCO's MIL brief asks people to think critically, check sources, use media safely, share ethically, and take part in public talk that is fair. In Philippine barangays, that moment is not a classroom. It is an SK information officer with a phone and a Facebook Page when a fake evacuation map hits the chat.

UNESCO notes that 85% of citizens worry about online disinformation, two-thirds of digital content creators do not systematically fact-check before sharing, and 80% of young people use AI tools multiple times a day. Fluent AI text makes rumors look official. SK already has the mandate. They lack a gate they can launch without a vendor.

## Solution

Pollux is an open-source aide. An SK officer creates an org, clones a template (flood, election rumor, or official notices), fills local facts, publishes, then commits before a link counts as SK-official. If the rumor is not in the pack, she refuses. Drafts stay invisible. Crisis facts never come from an LLM or the open web.

Seekers Guild is the youth community that stewards the project: feedback, template gaps, code, and partnerships, especially from seekers outside Manila. Guild is not required to launch. Guild is not the name on the SK Page. SK-published facts stay with that SK.

## How this is MIL (not a lecture)

- Think critically: a pause before official share.
- Spot fake news: not in the pack means not official. No fact-check model.
- Know the source: publisher, date, version on every item.
- Use media well: PWA, no scrape, minimize youth PII.
- Share ethically: human commit; refuse is first-class; no auto-post.
- Build a better society: SK public mandate made executable; Guild extends contribution beyond major hubs.

## Who it serves

Primary: Sangguniang Kabataan and LGU youth desks, including provinces. Secondary: young contributors who are not SK officers.

## Sustainability

Apache-2.0 code. CC-BY empty templates. SK term is one year; a pack they own can pass to the next officer. Seekers Guild keeps contribution open when a founder is not in the room. Paid DFY install for cities is later, not the contest story.

## Feasibility

Docs and confinement rules exist in this repo. v1 Must-Have is the canon desk, templates, launch checklist, paper card, and commit share. Optional inoculation drill is not required. Team ships a phone path: clone, publish, share, refuse. Paper card is an acceptable fallback if the hosted app is not live by video day.

## Inclusion

Designed for cheap phones and 3G. Paper QR card. Contribution rungs that do not require living in NCR. CLR consent remains a blocker before public launch to minors; contest demo uses 18+ SK officer persona (Mia, 19).

## Jury sentence (paste)

Pollux is an open-source aide SK councils launch themselves. Seekers Guild is the youth community that stewards it, especially seekers outside major hubs. Only a human SK officer can share a pack as official, so fluent rumors do not become government voice.

## What we will not claim

We will not claim we out-check the firehose. We will not ship a companion. We will not put Guild on the SK Page. We will not make a game the product.
