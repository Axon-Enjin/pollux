# UNESCO 3-minute rehearsal

Speak [pitch-pollux.md](../pitch-pollux.md) §8 against a timer. Hard cap 3:00. Target 2:30.

UNESCO video asks in this cut: story (Hook + Problem), solution (Solution + Proof), originality and impact (Close).

## Word count (scripted)

| Beat | Seconds | Words (approx) |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Story: the problem | 15 | 30 |
| Story: why it matters | 20 | 45 |
| Solution: what and who | 20 | 44 |
| Solution: how | 70 | 121 |
| Originality and impact | 25 | 39 |
| **Total** | **150** | **279** |

Measured word count of §8 spoken text: **279 words**. At 130 wpm that is **129 seconds** of speech. Leave the remaining ~21 seconds for hands on the print packet and run-of-show during Proof. If a timed read of the full text exceeds 2:40 before gestures, cut nothing from the jury sentence; trim Proof hall/court detail instead.

## One rehearsal pass

1. Print the packet pages in [../outreach-kit/print/README.md](../outreach-kit/print/README.md). Print [../outreach-kit/facilitator/3ds-run-of-show.md](../outreach-kit/facilitator/3ds-run-of-show.md) as the program guide. Do not use the F3 card as the product demo.
2. Put packet and run-of-show on the table. Student URL on the phone if you show it. Never [pollux-lesson](../assets/concept/pollux-lesson.png) if that file appears. No clone-fill-publish tap.
3. Start a 3:00 countdown. Speak §8 aloud once. Do not stop for mistakes unless you blow past 3:00.
4. Record that pass if it lands under 3:00. Burn [unesco-3min.srt](unesco-3min.srt) in your editor (CapCut, iMovie, or HandBrake).
5. Tick “Rehearsal done once” in the PITCH self-check.

## Camera notes

- Face for hook and close. Hands for open kit, one module (pause before share), and the student URL.
- On-screen words only: Saturday. They run the session. Open kit / Pause / Student URL.
- Quiet room. Phone mic 30cm off axis, or a lav.
- English VO, or local language plus these English subs.
