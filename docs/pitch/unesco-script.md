# UNESCO 2:30 pitch script

**Target:** 2:30. **Hard cap:** 3:00. About 130 words per minute.

**Spoken word count:** 268. At 130 wpm that is about 124 seconds of speech. Leave the remaining time in Solution for hands on the packet and the phone.

UNESCO video asks in this cut: story (Hook + Problem), solution (what, how, who), originality and impact (Value + Close).

## Pre-flight

Quiet room. Print packet and 3Ds run-of-show on the table. Student URL ready on the phone. Mia 19. Synthetic session only. No Guild logo as SK Page. No lesson or game UI. No official-pack tap. English VO, or local language with English subtitles. Do not add slides.

Table props: facilitator one-pager, learner handout, run-of-show. On-screen words, if any: Saturday. They run the session. Open kit / Pause / Student URL.

Cuts only on these five beats if you cannot do one take.

---

## Hook (0:00-0:08)

**Video:** [Face. Empty table. Phone in hand. No packet yet. On-screen: Saturday.]

**Audio:** Saturday outreach. Mia is nineteen, SK information officer. She has a phone and a Page. Empty table.

## Problem (0:08-0:35)

**Video:** [Face. Still of the blank table. Tarps or a covered court if you have them. No kit in frame yet.]

**Audio:** No agenda. No handouts. Nowhere for students after the tarps come down. Media and information literacy is the hour she runs this Saturday. Fluent AI makes rumors look official. SK already has the mandate. They have no vendor in the room. Youth desks outside Manila get the same blank morning. Doing nothing leaves the hour empty.

## Solution (0:35-2:05)

**Video:** [0:35-0:55 Face, then hands set packet and run-of-show on the table. On-screen: They run the session.] [0:55-2:05 Hands. Open the print packet. Clock pause before share from the learner page. Hold the phone with the student URL. On-screen: Open kit / Pause / Student URL. Pause for hands after Document, Demonstrate, Duplicate.]

**Audio:** Pollux is the open campaign kit an SK officer runs herself: modules, a program guide, and a site students return to after. Pre-made modules they can teach. A run-of-show and a logistics list they can tailor. They run the session. We pack the bag.

Watch the kit. This print packet is the demo. This page is the run-of-show. Mia opens both herself. Six modules. Forty-five minutes on a covered court. Half a day if they have the hall.

Document. Demonstrate. Duplicate. She writes the checklist. She shows pause before share once. The room copies from the handout. A fluent rumor hits. Hands stay down. They check the source. If it is not in the pack, it is not official. This hour is practice.

Students leave with a URL. They can open the site after the session.

## Value (2:05-2:18)

**Video:** [Face. Packet and run-of-show still on the table. Phone with the student URL visible. No Guild crest.]

**Audio:** Students keep a URL. The same bag works outside Manila. Nothing valuable is paywalled. Showcase both. Then let SK officers run Saturday without us.

## Close (2:18-2:30)

**Video:** [Face. Packet + run-of-show still. Hold the last look. No Guild crest. Pause after the last line.]

**Audio:** Pollux is the open campaign kit SK councils run themselves. Seekers Guild partners with youth desks, especially outside the big cities. Modules, program guide, and student access stay free.

## Claim audit (say only the Audio above)

Allowed: kit notes exist; v1 is the campaign kit; print packet and run-of-show are the demo; the hour is practice; demo persona is 18+; public camps with minors wait on consent; Apache-2.0 / CC-BY templates when they exist; materials stay free.

Forbidden: production-ready software; we fact-check the internet; kit completion launches an official pack; Guild publishes SK facts; we race rumor volume with a model; official-pack tap as the product; UNESCO climate statistics as Pollux results.
