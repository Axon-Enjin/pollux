## 1\. The MIL Index Approach

DW Akademie's Media and Information Literacy (MIL) model consists of five pillars, shown as a wheel: **Access, Analyze, Reflect, Create, Act**

- **Access** – Being able to use media technology to receive media messages and know where to find information.  
- **Analysis** – Being able to interpret and critically evaluate media messages based on one's own media knowledge.  
- **Reflection** – Critical self-examination of the information sources used and the impact certain communication can have.  
- **Creation** – Being able to create and compose messages to express ideas or share information.  
- **Action** – Putting MIL skills into practice for the benefit of the community and the individual.

**Discussion prompt:** In one sentence, describe how AI has changed your information ecosystem.

> "AI raises the stakes — our Heroes need new tools."

## The Villains

- **Deepfakes:** Fabricated media with misleading content produced using AI.  
- **Misinformation:** False information shared without intent to cause harm.  
- **Disinformation:** False information deliberately created or spread to cause harm.  
- **Malinformation:** Genuine information or subjective interpretation shared to deliberately cause harm.  
- **Cyber-harassment:** Intentionally humiliating, attacking, threatening, or verbally abusing individuals online.  
- **Doxxing:** Publicly exposing someone's private information (name, address, job, etc.).

### Zone 4 — Media Sharing & Attention Zone

**Villain pattern: Agitator** — engineered to capture emotions, spike reactions, and spread content fast, not always truthfully. *Patterns:* emotional triggers, viral loops, trending now, share pressure, speed over truth.

### Zone 5 — Data & Services Zone

**Villain pattern: Thief** — quietly collects, tracks, and personalizes to shape what you see, buy, and where you go; convenience costs your data. *Patterns:* tracking, profiling/personalisation, behaviour data, convenience trade-offs.

### Zone 6 — Media Creation Zone

**Villain pattern: Manipulator** — crafts convincing illusions through curated images, fabricated stories, and distorted reality. *Patterns:* synthetic media, reality blurring/half-truths, persuasion by design, curated narratives.

### Zone 7 — Identity & Interaction Zone

**Villain pattern: Bully** — uses pressure, exclusion, and attacks to make you doubt yourself and conform. *Patterns:* exclusion, harassment, dogpiling, identity attacks, pressure to conform.

**Discussion prompt:** Which villain do you encounter online?

## The Heroes

Four hero actions in response to the villains: **Create** (resisting manipulation), **Access/Reflect** (seeing clearly, examining self), **Analyze** (scrutinizing information), **Act** (collective action against negativity).

### Let's Access — Andre

Andre loved sharing his life online until a hack exposed his private info. He fought back by learning digital safety. As **Access**, he helps people lock down accounts, recover stolen identities, and protect privacy. **Access means being able to:**

- Find reliable information online, not just what the algorithm feeds you  
- Understand how AI search, filters, and recommendation systems work  
- Protect personal data and digital identity from misuse  
- Use tools and tech safely, without being excluded

### Let's Analyse — Amina

Amina once got trapped in fake news and algorithm bubbles, almost losing touch with reality — until she discovered critical thinking. As **Analyse**, she helps people question sources, check facts, and see the bigger picture. **Analyse means being able to:**

- Spot misinformation, disinformation, and AI-generated fakes (like deepfakes)  
- Compare sources and detect bias in media or AI systems  
- Understand how algorithms shape what you see  
- Think critically instead of scrolling mindlessly

### Let's Create — Caslav

Caslav was bullied online and compared to others until he lost his confidence. He rebuilt himself through creativity and now teaches others to do the same. As **Create**, he empowers people to express themselves in positive, original ways. **Create means being able to:**

- Make your own content (posts, videos, art, podcasts) responsibly  
- Use AI tools for creativity without losing your own voice  
- Respect copyright, ethics, and cultural values  
- Share messages that add value, not noise or harm

### Let's Reflect — Reaksmey

Reaksmey chased likes and trends until she burned out. She learned mindfulness and found her real self again. As **Reflect**, she helps people stay balanced, authentic, and thoughtful in digital spaces. **Reflect means being able to:**

- Recognize how media and AI affect your emotions and mental health  
- Think about ethics and values before sharing or creating content  
- Stay true to yourself instead of chasing algorithms  
- Balance online and offline life

### Let's Act — Akosua

Akosua watched cancel culture destroy an innocent friend. She turned her anger into action, learning advocacy and justice skills. As **Act**, she speaks up, defends victims, and rallies communities for change. **Act means being able to:**

- Take part in society and stand up against injustice (online and offline)  
- Use AI and media tools to organize for positive change  
- Report abuse, harassment, or harmful AI use  
- Be an active, responsible digital citizen

### MIL Champions

Together, Access, Analyse, Create, Reflect, and Act form a powerful team that turned their struggles into strengths. Their mission: fight misinformation, protect identities, and inspire safe, smart, and creative use of media, especially in the age of AI.
